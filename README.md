# Parxel
A pixel art drawing app! Made with Next.js.

<hr />

## How to Use
### The Landing Page
On the landing page, you have two options:
1. Create a new project by typing in the grid width and grid height (or leaving them at their defaults) and clicking "Continue"
2. Continue an existing Parxel project by uploading a .parxel file and clicking "Continue"

### The Tool Screen
- **Changing your color:** Use the color picker next to the "Paint Color:" label.
- **Changing your tool:** Parxel has two tools: a "Draw" tool and an "Erase" tool. Click on a tool's icon to switch to that tool.
- **Zooming in or out:** Click the "⌕+" to zoom in, and click the "⌕-" to zoom out.
- **Toggling gridline visibility:** Click the ⌗.
- **Undo:** Click the ↩.
- **Clear:** Click the 🗑️.
- **Saving as SVG:** Click "Download SVG"
- **Saving as .parxel file:** Click "Download .parxel"
